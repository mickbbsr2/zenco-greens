Expert Planting Guide for Fruit Trees in Bhubaneswar

Recommended Potting Mix

Mix 40% garden red soil / alluvial soil, 30% well-decomposed cow dung manure (or vermicompost), 20% cocopeat (to retain moisture during hot summer breezes), and 10% neem cake powder + bone meal for root strength.

Summer & Cyclone Care

Mulch the top 2 inches with dry leaves or hay in April-May to prevent moisture loss. The wide base and flexible HDPE body withstand high winds during Kalbaisakhi and coastal storms without tipping.
